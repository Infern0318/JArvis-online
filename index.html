App icon

An iPhone web app can't change its Home Screen icon by itself (only native apps can). Trick that avoids deleting the app: save the icon below, then in the Shortcuts app create a shortcut “Open URL” with your Jarvis address, tap ⓘ → Add to Home Screen, tap the icon and choose the saved photo. To change it later, just edit that shortcut's icon.

How Jarvis talks to you

Location & weather

Jarvis only uses your position when you ask about the weather or directions, and never saves it. Without it you can still ask about a named city.

Always listening

While Jarvis is open, on any tab, saying “Jarvis” brings you back here and he answers. The screen stays on. Safari can't listen when the screen is locked or you are in another app (iPhone rule). AI providers (they work together)

Add several free keys. Jarvis uses them in this order; if one hits its free limit or fails, he automatically switches to the next. Photos go to Groq or Gemini, long documents to the provider with the biggest capacity.
Privacy: your keys, notes and flashcards stay on this device (browser storage). Your messages are sent to the providers you added. Speech recognition is done by Apple's Safari.
